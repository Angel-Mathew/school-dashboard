# School Dashboard

A dashboard for a school in Sharjah. It covers students, teachers, buses and the admissions process across the 2024, 2025 and 2026 academic years.

## The problem
A school has to manage places in classes, transport and admissions all at once. I built this to give the school one view of all three.

## What you can explore
- Headline numbers: 979 students, 52 teachers, 14 buses, 4 clinic staff and 110 seats remaining
- Enrolment by class
- Gender split (about 49 percent female, 51 percent male)
- Bus routes by area on a map
- Seats remaining by class
- An admissions funnel from enquiry to admission

## What I found
In the admissions funnel, 1,193 enquiries led to 833 applications, 659 assessments, 549 offers and 470 admissions. That means about 39 percent of enquiries end up admitted and about 86 percent of offers are accepted.
